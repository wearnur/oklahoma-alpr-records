A live index of automated license-plate readers and the contracts behind them.

Cameras are already on OpenStreetMap. The gap is the packet: who paid, how much, how long footage is kept, who else can query it.

This site does not store plates, faces, or vehicle paths. It retrieves the public record at the speed a clerk would, if the clerk had already filed the request.
